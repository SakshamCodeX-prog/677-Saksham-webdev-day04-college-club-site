Welcome to the Coding Club

More than a club.
A place to code.

Campus Connect Coding Club at Invertis University, Bareilly, is a student community focused on programming, technology, creativity, and learning together. We encourage students to explore coding, share ideas, and build exciting projects.

Why we’re here

Learn, build, and grow together.

Learning programming becomes easier when students work together. Our club provides opportunities to improve coding skills, explore new technologies, and solve real-world problems.

Whether you are a beginner or already have programming experience, you can share ideas, participate in activities, and learn from other students.
